# SSP-105: Author and compile the grey-box High Street (80×60) for Tuesday's NPCs

**Milestone:** [M3 Living Street](../README.md#m3) · **Type:** feature · **Size:** L · **Areas:** gameplay, tooling, test

## Summary

Write assets-src/levels/high_street.map.txt and high_street.level.json as a playable grey-box with placeholder art, giving Tuesday's buses, market traders, litterbugs, businessmen, pigeons and A-boards a home, compiled by `npm run levels`.

## Context

LEVEL_DESIGN §5.2 (all values initial):

Layout (80×60)
- High Street spine: 4-tile carriageway (two lanes plus a bus box at each stop), 2-tile pavements with A-boards, litter bins and planters, two zebras (`z`, ≥ 3 tiles from junction mouths) and two bus stops (`b`, 3–4 tiles, with a `u` shelter behind).
- Market Square: about 20×14 tiles of cobbles `:`, 12 stall pitches (`m`) in two rows, 3-tile drivable aisles.
- Station Road: one-way loop (`>`/`<`) behind the shops, with service yards, wheelie bins and 2 skips.
- Ginnels: 2-tile drivable alleys every 25–35 m.
- Depot on the north edge off Station Road (≥ 2 depot bays `$`, @ inside depot.rect).
- Heights: 3 storeys on side streets, 4–5 on the High Street, which is 8 tiles facade to facade (lean rule: ≥ 4 storeys need ≥ 6 tiles).
- Theme IDs: late morning, clear; mus.high_street, amb.town_morning (IDs only in M3).

Density
- 200 initial litter items (+ about 60 dynamic, ~230 LU). Table: crisp 20, butts 18, can 12, bottle 10, chip wrapper 10, newspaper 8, banana skin 8, glass bottle 6, pizza box 4, bin bag 4.
- Grime 11,000 cells (dense at bus stops, chippy, pitches). Stains 30 stain.gum, 6 stain.milkshake.
- Skips / hydrants 2 / 4. Power-ups 2 from the pool (tea, super suction, water can). Frontages / pledges 64 / 12.
- Lost Property collect.lost_wallet between stalls; Golden Crisp in the far bus box (L041 requires both).
- Par (LEVEL_DESIGN §6.2): 1★ 89 s, 2★ 150 s, 3★ 184 s, 100% 289 s; par.loadout upg.hopper L1 (140 LU).

NPCs: shopper 18 (26 during the 120–180 s wave), market_trader 12 (one per pitch), businessman 5, litterbug 3 (+2 at 90 s), old_woman 1, old_man_frame 1, 2 pigeon flocks of 6–10 at chip wrappers in the square. Peak 51.
Traffic: 12 moving (bus 2 every 45 s from 20 s; taxi 2; bicycle 2; cars 6 from hatchback/saloon/van_white) plus 14 parked in Station Road loading bays. *Note (SSP-090):* a parked car takes a 2-tile bay (a run of n `p` tiles holds ⌊n / 2⌋ cars), so 14 parked needs 28 bay tiles at parkedFill 0.5.
Events: `market` (continuous), `market_close` at 180 s (about 40 items over 20 s).

Validation: every L-rule in LEVEL_DESIGN §10.3, including L005/L007 and L020/L021 (dump within 200 m, water within 150 m). Run the §12 checklist except the human 3★ replay (M6). Grey-box: placeholder facades and tiles; real shopfronts and signs are M4.

## Scope

- Author the ASCII map and level.json (buildings, anchors, bus route, pledges, litter, grime, stains, knockables, powerups, collectibles, npcs, traffic, events, par).
- Compile, commit public/assets/levels/high_street.json, paste the par report into the PR.
- Add a 3,600-tick seed-1 smoke replay tests/replay/replays/m3_high_street.json (input log generated by a committed script) so `npm run test:replay` covers the district.
- Read a full-map screenshot (`?test=1&scene=district.high_street&freeze=1`) for readability.

## Out of scope

- Final High Street art, signs and decals (M4).
- Final balance and the human 3★ replay (M6, LEVEL_DESIGN Q7).
- mus.high_street audio (M5).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `npm run levels` compiles high_street with zero errors; any warnings are explained in the PR.
- [ ] The par report shows peak NPC ≤ 80 (target ~51) and peak vehicles ≤ 30.
- [ ] `npm run assets:verify` passes with the compiled JSON committed.
- [ ] m3_high_street.json passes `npm run test:replay` with no invariant failures.
- [ ] The full-map screenshot was opened with Read; the PR notes grime zones are visible and no hazards or pledges are hidden under roof lean.
- [ ] The committed `public/assets/levels/high_street.json` carries `"music": "mus.high_street"` and `"ambience": "amb.town_morning"`.
